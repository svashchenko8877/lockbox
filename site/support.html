Lockbox – Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/lockbox/issues and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Please do not post any private content.

Restore your purchase

Open Settings in Lockbox and tap Restore Purchase.

Forgot how to unlock?

Lockbox opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode. If you delete the app or reset your iPhone, the vault cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
