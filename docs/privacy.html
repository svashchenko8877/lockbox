Lockbox – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Data we collect

Lockbox does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Photos, videos, notes and files you add are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and stored only on your device. The encryption key is protected by the Secure Enclave and can only be used after you authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The vault is excluded from iCloud and device backups. We cannot access or recover your data.

Permissions

Camera and microphone are used only if you capture a photo or video directly into the vault. Photo library access is used only if you choose to delete originals after importing. Face ID is used only to unlock the vault. Nothing is sent anywhere.

In-app purchase

The optional "Full Vault" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

Lockbox collects no information from anyone, including children.
